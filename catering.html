Got questions about booking, logistics, or customizing our mobile shave carts for your special day? Find all the answers below.

Our mobile carts require an 8ft x 8ft level footprint. Each cart is completely self-contained with onboard whisper-quiet battery power or can plug into a standard 110V 15-amp household outlet. We set up effortlessly on outdoor patios, lawns, beachside venues, or inside banquet halls and ballrooms.

We recommend booking 3 to 6 weeks in advance, especially for weekend dates and peak spring/summer months. For large corporate galas or weddings, booking 2–3 months ahead guarantees your preferred time slot and custom cart branding.
